- WoW Forever: glancing chance and damage lost come from the game itself.

## 1.4.0

- First public release.
- Your attacks, melee and ranged, and attacks on you, as one colored bar in roll order with a table of the numbers.
- Opens from a minimap button, the minimap's addon menu, or `/attacktable`.
- WoW Forever: monsters below level 10 dodge, parry and block less, in step with their level; monsters do not crit you while their weapon skill is less than 10 above your defense.
